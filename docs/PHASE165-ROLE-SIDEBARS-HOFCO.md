## Role-based sidebar engine
profiles.hq_function holds one of the nine HQ roles (founder, hofco, secretary,
admin, programmes, comms, finance, it, qa). Accounts → "HQ function" sets it;
defaults: super_admin / executive_director → founder, hq_team → hofco. Each role
gets its own sidebar from assets/hq-sidebars.json, the modules of her Parts A to
I in her order (Founder 13, HOFCO 22, Secretary 15, Administration 16, Programmes
16, Communications 20, Finance 16, IT 12, QA 9). Every entry opens the WDOS screen
that holds that function; a module that does not exist yet (Finance and its
sub-modules, Authority matrix, Risk register, Media library, Social/campaigns,
Integrations console, Readiness register) opens a "Scheduled module" page that
states its purpose and status. Dead-link check: 306 links, 0 dead.
Opening screen by role: Founder → Command Centre (strategic); HOFCO, Secretary and
the Directors → the HOFCO reference screen until their own screens land.

## Reference Screen 1 — HOFCO dashboard (#/ for hofco)
Header: welcome, role, motto, reporting period (Today / week / month / quarter /
YTD), network selector (All / WGMN / WNNN); both recompute everything.
Row 1 tiles (all clickable): countries operational, leadership coverage %, active
leaders (30 days), priority vacancies (CR/DCR/state), reports outstanding,
activities this period.
Row 2 Attention Required: Issue · Network · Country · Level · Owner · Priority ·
Deadline · Action, generated by rules: CR seat vacant (critical), red/critical
issue (critical), CR inactive 30+ days (high), report overdue (high), support
request unresolved 3+ days (high), task overdue, activation past its end date,
appointment not accepted 14+ days (medium), engagement declining (normal).
Second click opens the record; third click acts there.
Row 3 My Work Queue: approvals, requests, reports to review, follow-ups I
assigned, decisions, escalated, due today, due this week, overdue, returned,
awaiting verification.
Row 4 WGMN vs WNNN: countries with leaders, leaders, active, members, open
applications, reporting compliance, activities, at-risk.
Row 5 Country performance: coverage, vacancies, states led, reports, activities,
engagement %, status (On track / Attention / At risk / Intervention); row click →
Country 360 (#/country/:id).
Row 6 engagement summary (active, consistently engaged, declining, at risk,
inactive, support open, milestones this month); Row 7 reporting & evidence; Row 8
activities & programme implementation (upcoming, today, completed, delayed,
evidence pending, participants, women reached); Row 9 recent network activity;
quick actions (review country, approve/review request, assign follow-up, send
directive, critical issues, vacancies, reports).
