## HQ sidebar = the founder's "Recommended WDOS Central Admin Sidebar"
Twelve collapsible sections in her order (Command · People & Structure · Networks ·
Recruitment & Leadership · Operations · Accountability · Programmes · Engagement ·
Safety & Support · WODDI Ecosystem · Insights · Administration) plus Resources, with
every entry she listed. Each entry opens the WDOS screen that holds that function
today (dead-link check: 167 links, 0 dead). Where the entry's own screen is not yet
built, it opens the closest one holding that information:
- Immediate Attention → Reporting dashboard exceptions; Approvals & Decisions →
  Recruitment approvals; Expansion & Strengthening, Country Scorecards, Analytics →
  Network Command; Leadership Coverage / Replacement & Succession → Structure
  (vacant); Appointments → Accounts (acceptance pending); Seat Management → Leaders
  seat page; Leadership Packs → Library; Online Applications / Activation Centre /
  Candidate Review → Recruitment (filters); Country Operations → Issues;
  Operational Readiness → Tasks (HQ); Weekly Reports → Review; Monthly
  Consolidation / Executive Reports → Archive; Evidence Library / Activity Reporting
  → Operational records; Impact Tracker → the previous HQ dashboard; Communications
  Centre → Announcements; Volunteer Engagement → Spotlight review; Complaints →
  Cases; WODDI Institute → Outreach; WODDI Community → Members; Integrations /
  Workflow / Notifications / Languages / System Settings → Settings; Forms & Data
  Standards → Content Studio; Audit & Compliance / Security & Audit Logs → Doctor;
  Users & Access / Roles & Permissions → Accounts. Meeting Room, Messages and Ask
  WODDI are kept (Operations, Engagement, Resources) because HQ uses them daily.

## Leader profiles are HQ-maintained
A seated leader can no longer change her photo or edit her profile; the Profile tab
is read-only with "maintained by Headquarters · Ask HQ to update something"
(Support). HQ edits via Accounts (Edit details / Photo). Members who joined through
the form keep their own editing.
