# PHASE 166 - Approved reference screens 2 and 3 in code; network titles
Build v75.0 · db 128 · 10 Sep 2026
The founder approved the three reference screens (Figma: WDOS Reference Screens —
Founder UI Specification). Screen 1 (HOFCO) shipped in v74.0; this build ships
Screens 2 and 3 in the approved row order.

## Screen 2 — Country Representative / Country Lead (country- and state-level seats)
Welcome band (network wordmark, flag, ID, designation in the network's title) ·
six overview tiles (states with a coordinator + coverage %, priority vacancies,
my tasks with overdue, HQ directives unread, my referrals, monthly reports in with
due date and outstanding) · Attention required generated from her own structure
(report overdue per state, vacant seats, inactive or never-signed-in leaders,
overdue tasks, HQ announcements to read), each line opening its record ·
Recruitment & referrals with the two pathways (referral route, online applicant
route) and the HQ-only note · Activities & programmes (meetings and activities in
30 days, next meeting, Submit activity report) · Volunteer engagement of her
leaders (active / quiet / inactive / never) · My structure: the full country panel
(charts, states, coordinators with responsiveness, KPIs / Message, drill-down).

## Screen 3 — Chapter Coordinator / Chapter Lead, and members (mobile-first)
What needs doing next (report due, next activity, my tasks) as large touch
actions · My chapter / My network (members, activities in 30 days, referrals) ·
Announcements · Required learning with progress · five quick actions (add member,
submit activity report, refer a leader, view next activity, request support) ·
Get help naming the supervisor.

## Network titles
WGMN: Country Representative, State Coordinator, LGA Coordinator, Chapter
Coordinator. WNNN: Country Lead, State / Regional Lead, LGA / District Lead,
Chapter Lead (core/titles.js), used in the welcome band and context line.

## Unchanged
Every previous card is still reachable on its tab (My Learning, My Network, My
Impact, Tasks, Meetings, Announcements); the home screen is what changed.
